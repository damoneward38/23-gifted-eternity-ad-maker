User clicks: GENERATE VIDEO
       ↓
Create VideoJob (`status: "queued"`, `attempts: 0`)
       ↓
Worker picks job (`status: "planning"`)
       ↓
Neural Core creates screenplay (Beat analysis & screenplay generation)
       ↓
Scene planner (Scene breakdown, narrative objectives, lighting & color grades)
       ↓
Shot planner (Focal lengths, lens models, camera motion profiles, seed locks)
       ↓
Video provider (`status: "generating"`, dispatching to Runway Gen-3, Luma Ray-2, SVD, Sora, or FFmpeg)
       ↓
Actual clip (Temporal motion synthesis with continuous frame movement)
       ↓
QC (Temporal stability, motion artifact checks, frame jitter analysis)
       ↓
If failed → retry (Scene attempt counter incremented with automatic regeneration)
       ↓
Next scene (Iterates until all scenes pass QC)
       ↓
FFmpeg (`status: "compositing"`, non-linear EDL assembly & transition rendering)
       ↓
Audio (Voiceover dialogue stem alignment + multi-track music/SFX mixing)
       ↓
Final QC (`status: "quality_check"`, LUFS loudness normalization & zero-mock SHA-256 validation)
       ↓
MP4 (`status: "completed"`, faststart streamable master video output)
